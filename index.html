AnimateO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

AnimateO is an Android application that provides a customizable dynamic-island-style overlay and related visual features. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information AnimateO may access, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

2. Information We Collect

AnimateO does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties.

AnimateO may access certain information on your device when you explicitly grant the relevant Android permissions. This information is used only to provide the application's features and is processed locally on your device.

3. Notification Access

If you grant AnimateO notification access, the application may read notifications displayed by other applications. This allows AnimateO to provide features such as displaying notification-related information through its interface.

Notification information is processed locally on your device. AnimateO does not transmit notification content to the developer's servers or sell or share notification content with third parties.

4. Accessibility Service

AnimateO may use Android's Accessibility Service when you explicitly enable it. This permission is used to support the application's overlay and interface functionality, including detecting relevant changes on the device so that AnimateO can provide its visual features.

AnimateO does not use the Accessibility Service to collect personal information or transmit information to external servers.

5. Display Over Other Apps

AnimateO may request permission to display over other applications. This permission is required to display the AnimateO interface and dynamic-island-style elements on top of other applications.

This permission does not cause AnimateO to collect or transmit information from other applications.

6. Data Storage

AnimateO may store application settings and preferences locally on your device so that the application can remember your configuration. This information is not uploaded to the developer or shared with third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

AnimateO does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

AnimateO does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Since AnimateO does not transmit personal information to the developer or third parties, no personal information is collected through the application for advertising or profiling purposes.

9. Security

Because information accessed by AnimateO is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers, AnimateO does not maintain a remote database containing your personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to AnimateO or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or AnimateO's privacy practices, you can contact the developer at:

Email: aarb.icecream@gmail.com
