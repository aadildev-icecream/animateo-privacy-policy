Animate O Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Animate O is an Android application that provides a customizable dynamic-island-style overlay and related visual features. This Privacy Policy explains what information Animate O may access, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

2. Information We Collect

Animate O does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties.

Animate O may access certain information on your device when you explicitly grant the relevant Android permissions. This information is used only to provide the application's features and is processed locally on your device.

3. Notification Access

If you grant Animate O notification access, the application may read notifications displayed by other applications. This allows Animate O to provide features such as displaying notification-related information through its interface.

Notification information is processed locally on your device. Animate O does not transmit notification content to the developer's servers or sell or share notification content with third parties.

4. Accessibility Service

Animate O may use Android's Accessibility Service when you explicitly enable it. This permission is used to support the application's overlay and interface functionality, including detecting relevant changes on the device so that Animate O can provide its visual features.

Animate O does not use the Accessibility Service to collect personal information or transmit information to external servers.

5. Display Over Other Apps

Animate O may request permission to display over other applications. This permission is required to display the Animate O interface and dynamic-island-style elements on top of other applications.

This permission does not cause Animate O to collect or transmit information from other applications.

6. Data Storage

Animate O may store application settings and preferences locally on your device so that the application can remember your configuration. This information is not uploaded to the developer or shared with third parties.

7. Third-Party Services

Animate O does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Animate O does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Animate O does not transmit personal information to the developer or third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

9. Security

Information accessed by Animate O is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer's servers. Animate O does not maintain a remote database containing your personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Animate O or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
